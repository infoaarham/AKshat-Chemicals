Frequently Asked

Before you pick up the phone.

What is your minimum order quantity?
MOQ varies by product — typically one drum or bag for APIs and one drum/IBC for solvents. Tanker products can be quoted from part-loads. Ask us for the exact MOQ on your molecule.
Do you supply a Certificate of Analysis?
Yes — every consignment travels with a batch-matched CoA, and MSDS is available for all products. Pre-shipment samples can be arranged for approval on request.
How fast can you quote and dispatch?
Firm quotations go out within 24 working hours. Ex-stock material dispatches within 1–3 working days of PO; indent/import items are quoted with a clear lead time.
Can you source products not in the catalogue?
Yes. Our import lines and vendor network regularly source molecules and grades outside the published list against your specification and target price.
Which regions do you deliver to?
All of India — our Bhiwandi warehouse connects directly to national trunk routes, and we routinely ship to Gujarat, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, NCR, MP and beyond.
