Our Story

From one desk to a national supply network.

Akshat Chemicals began in 2007 as a single trading desk in Andheri East with one operating rule: quote honestly, deliver exactly what was quoted, and be there after the invoice. The rule has not changed — the scale has. Today we run direct import lines from audited overseas manufacturers, warehousing at Bhiwandi, and a dispatch network reaching every industrial cluster in India.

  • Promoter-led — the founder personally oversees pricing, imports and key accounts.
  • Head office at Citi Point, Andheri East; warehousing at Anant and Company, Bhiwandi.
  • Pharmacopoeia, commercial and industrial grades with CoA on every batch.
  • Import-scale sourcing across domestic and overseas vendor networks.

Leadership

The founder still answers the phone.

Uday Mehta founded Akshat Chemicals in 2007 and has personally led every import line, price negotiation and key account since. Nearly two decades in the chemical trade built a simple leadership philosophy: a supplier is only as good as his last delivery.

  • Customer-first: supply is engineered around the buyer’s plant, not the seller’s stock.
  • Values: honest quotations, batch-true documentation, commitments kept in writing and in spirit.
  • Experience: 18+ years across pharma, solvents and speciality imports — cycles, shortages and all.
  • Vision: a house Indian manufacturers rely on for an ever-wider range at import-scale pricing.
