About Us

Two decades of chemistry. One standard of trust.

Akshat Chemicals was founded in Mumbai in 2007 as an importer and dealer of chemicals and bulk drugs. Today we supply pharmaceutical, veterinary, food, cosmetic and industrial manufacturers across India.

2007Founded in Mumbai — promoter-run to this day.
Our Story

From one desk to 43 molecules.

What began as a small trading desk grew on a single rule: quote honestly, deliver exactly what was quoted, and be there after the invoice. A thousand-plus clients later, the rule hasn't changed.

  • Promoter-led — Uday Mehta, Brijesh Shah and Paras Gandhi handle key accounts personally.
  • Head office at Citi Point, Andheri East; warehousing at Bhiwandi.
  • IP / USP / BP Vet material with CoA on every batch.
  • Import-scale sourcing across domestic and overseas vendor networks.
